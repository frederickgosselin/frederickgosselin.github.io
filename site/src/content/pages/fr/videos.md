**Reconfiguration of a poroelastic system**

<iframe loading="lazy" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/8169002" width="500" height="281" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe>

  
Poroelastic system (a Hasbro Koosh ball) subjected to a stream of air varying from 0 to 30 meters per second in a wind tunnel. Like a tree subjected to wind, this poroelastic system becomes more streamlined as the drag load increases.

**Reconfiguration of a flexible rectangular plate**

<iframe loading="lazy" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/8176667" width="500" height="313" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe>

  
As the flow speed inside the wind tunnel is increased from 0 to 20 meters per second, the flexible rectangular plate bends down. As it does so, it becomes more streamlined and reduces its cross-sectional area.  
See the paper “Drag reduction by reconfiguration of flexible plates” for more information.

**Reconfiguration of a circular plate cut along many radii**

<iframe loading="lazy" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/8176539" width="500" height="313" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe>

  
As the flow speed in the wind tunnel is increased from 0 to 20 meters per second, the sectors of the disk bend down, hence making the object more streamlined and reducing its cross-sectional area.  
See the paper “Drag reduction by reconfiguration of flexible plates” for more information.
