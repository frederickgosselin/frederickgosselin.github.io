[Sampada Bodkhe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sampadabodkhe/)‘s PhD work on 3D printing of piezo-electric sensors has been selected by [Quebec Science magazine in its Top-10 Discoveries of the Year 2018!](https://www.quebecscience.qc.ca/decouverte2018/) The breakthrough allows 3D printing at room temperature of a deformation sensor and its electrodes in one step. We demonstrate how this can be used to make smart clothes that measure breathing or movement. We also demonstrate how one can print embedded sensors on a miniature drone wing allowing to monitor in real time its vibrations.

Visit the Québec Science website to vote for her invention and make it the Top discovery of 2018 in Quebec!
