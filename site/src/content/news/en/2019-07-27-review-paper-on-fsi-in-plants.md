My review paper on the “[Mechanics of a plant in fluid flow](https://academic.oup.com/jxb/article/70/14/3533/5518960?guestAccessKey=de334c3d-2366-4c77-98c2-2d35a0bcf6d0)” is out in the *Journal of Experimental Botany*. This review covers the statics and dynamics of plant–fluid interactions, e.g., the static reconfiguration of a plant deforming under fluid flow, and also its dynamic swaying and flapping. It covers the mechanics at play in terrestrial plants as well as aquatic plants and seaweeds. Biological implications are highlighted and ideas for future research avenues are suggested.
