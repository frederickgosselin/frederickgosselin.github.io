Our work on the cover of JFM

Our work on [the reconfiguration of flexible plates](http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0022112009993673) was featured on the [cover page of the Journal of Fluid Mechanics](http://dx.doi.org/doi:10.1017/S0022112010001321).

The JFM edition of may 10th 2010 shows three photographs of circula disks cut along many radii bending in the wind.
